Privacy Policy — Police Out

Package ID: com.policeout.game · Effective Date: November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Police Out ("the App") handles user data. We designed Police Out to be playable offline without requiring an account, while offering optional cloud sync for players who choose to sign in.

1. Information We Collect

  • Guest / Offline Mode: When playing as a guest, no personal information is collected or transmitted. Your game progress, stars, unlocked themes, and audio preferences are stored locally on your device.
  • Optional Cloud Account (Firebase Auth & Firestore): If you choose to create an account or sign in using Google Sign-In or Email & Password, we store your display name, email address, optional profile photo URL, and gameplay progress (solved stages, star ratings, achievements, and daily challenge scores) to enable cross-device sync and global leaderboards.

2. How We Use Information

  • To save and synchronize your game progress across devices.
  • To display your chosen display name and score on optional Daily Challenge and Global Leaderboards.
  • To authenticate your account and allow password setup or recovery.

3. Advertising, Analytics & Data Sharing

  • No Ads: Police Out contains zero third-party advertisements or ad-tracking SDKs.
  • No Data Selling: We never sell, rent, or trade your personal data to third parties.
  • Infrastructure Providers: Optional cloud login and progress storage are powered by Google Firebase (Firebase Authentication & Cloud Firestore).

4. Permissions

The Android application only requests the standard INTERNET permission solely for optional cloud authentication, progress synchronization, and leaderboard updates. All 360 puzzle levels and audio work offline.

5. Data Retention & Deletion

You may stop using cloud sync at any time by signing out inside the App, or clear local data from your device settings. To request permanent deletion of your cloud account and stored progress, please email us at hamzahussien2357@gmail.com.

6. Children's Privacy

Police Out is suitable for general audiences and does not knowingly collect personal data from children under the age of 13 without parental consent. Guest mode requires no personal data whatsoever.

7. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, contact the developer at: hamzahussien2357@gmail.com
